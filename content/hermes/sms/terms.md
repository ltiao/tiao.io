## SMS Terms

Louis Tiao operates Hermes household texts, an optional service for invited household members. Text HERMES to the number on your invitation and reply YES to confirm enrollment.

You receive one reminder per appointment. You may also receive occasional requests for input explicitly requested by the other household member. You can text questions about your own appointments, household plans, tasks and bookings. Message frequency varies with your schedule and requests. Message and data rates may apply.

**Reply STOP to opt out.** After one confirmation, messages to your number stop. **Reply HELP for help** or email Louis at hermes@tiao.io.
