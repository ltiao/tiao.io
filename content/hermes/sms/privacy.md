This policy applies only to the Hermes household SMS program operated by Louis Tiao. For use of this website, see the [website privacy policy](/privacy/).

Louis Tiao operates Hermes household texts. When you enroll, the program uses your mobile number and your enrollment and opt-out responses to determine whether to send you messages. It uses your messages and the calendar, task or booking details needed to answer your questions or remind you of your own appointments.

Twilio and mobile carriers process numbers and messages to deliver texts. When another household member asks for your input, Hermes tells you who will receive your answer and where it will go before you reply. If the request began in a channel with people outside the household, the answer goes privately to the requester.

We do not sell phone numbers, SMS consent information or message content. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not share these details for solicitation.

For privacy questions, email Louis at hermes@tiao.io.
