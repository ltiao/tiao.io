Louis Tiao operates Hermes for invited members of his household. Participation is optional.

To sign up, text **HERMES** to **(929) 552-2905**. Read the program details in the reply, then text **YES** to confirm. Each person enrolls from their own phone.

You’ll get one reminder per appointment and occasional texts when your spouse asks for your input. Before you answer, Hermes will tell you who will receive your response and where. You can also text Hermes about your own appointments, household plans, tasks and bookings.

Message frequency varies with your schedule and requests. Message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to opt out or **HELP** for help. For support, email Louis at [hermes@tiao.io](mailto:hermes@tiao.io).
