This is a personal academic website. It does not collect personal data of
its own. Two third-party services run on the site, described below.

## Server logs

The site is served from a third-party host, which may automatically record
standard access information (IP address, user agent, request timestamp,
referrer) for security and operational purposes. That data is governed by
the host's privacy policy.

## Analytics

The site uses [Google Analytics 4](https://policies.google.com/privacy) to
collect aggregate, anonymous information about visits — page views, rough
geography, device and browser type, referral source, and similar
behavioural signals. IP addresses are anonymised before storage. No data
is used for advertising or sold to third parties.

GA4 sets cookies in your browser. You can opt out site-wide by installing
the [Google Analytics opt-out
add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout), or by enabling Do Not
Track or your browser's tracking-protection features.

## Comments

Comments on blog posts are powered by [Giscus](https://giscus.app/), which
uses GitHub Discussions as the backing store. To post a comment you need a
GitHub account and must authorise the Giscus app. Your comment, GitHub
username, and avatar are stored as a discussion entry on the public
repository associated with this site. The relevant privacy policies are
[GitHub's](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
and [Giscus's](https://github.com/giscus/giscus/blob/main/PRIVACY-POLICY.md).
You can edit or delete your own comments via GitHub at any time.

Reading posts does not require a GitHub account. The Giscus widget is
loaded from `giscus.app` and may set cookies for session purposes.

## External links

Pages on this site link to third-party services such as GitHub, Google
Scholar, and the personal sites of collaborators. Following any external
link takes you out of the scope of this policy and into the privacy
practices of the destination, which I do not control.
