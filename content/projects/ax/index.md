[Ax](https://ax.dev/) is an open-source platform for adaptive experimentation
developed by Meta's Adaptive Experimentation team. It provides a unified
interface for Bayesian optimization, multi-fidelity, multi-objective, and
constrained optimization, built on top of [BoTorch](https://botorch.org/).

I contribute to Ax as part of my work at Meta, with a particular focus on
sample-efficient methods for hyperparameter tuning, capacity management, and
scaling-law-based modeling. Co-first author on *Ax: A Platform for Adaptive
Experimentation* (AutoML 2025).
