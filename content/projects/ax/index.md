[Ax](https://ax.dev/) is an open-source platform for [adaptive
experimentation](/tags/adaptive-experimentation/) developed by Meta's Adaptive
Experimentation team. It provides a unified interface for [Bayesian
optimization](/tags/bayesian-optimization/),
[multi-fidelity](/tags/multi-fidelity-optimization/), multi-objective, and
constrained optimization, built on top of [BoTorch](https://botorch.org/).

I contribute to Ax as part of my work at Meta, with a particular focus on
sample-efficient methods for [hyperparameter
tuning](/tags/hyperparameter-optimization/), capacity management, and
scaling-law-based modeling. Co-first author on [*Ax: A Platform for Adaptive
Experimentation*]({{< relref "/publications/ax-platform" >}}) (AutoML 2025).
