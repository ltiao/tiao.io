[BORE](https://github.com/ltiao/bore) is the reference implementation of
*Bayesian Optimization by Density-Ratio Estimation* (Tiao et al., ICML 2021).
It recasts the acquisition function in BO as a probabilistic classification
problem, sidestepping the analytical-tractability constraints of conventional
surrogate-based methods.

Developed with Aaron Klein.
