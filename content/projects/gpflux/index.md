[GPflux](https://github.com/secondmind-labs/GPflux) is a TensorFlow/Keras
framework for Deep Gaussian Processes, developed at Secondmind Labs.
It builds on [GPflow](https://www.gpflow.org/) and exposes Deep GP layers as
familiar Keras building blocks, making it easier to compose deep probabilistic
architectures.

Contributed during my doctoral student researcher appointment at Secondmind
Labs, alongside Vincent Dutordoir, ST John, and other members of the lab.
