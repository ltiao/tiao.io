[GPflux](https://github.com/secondmind-labs/GPflux) is a TensorFlow/Keras
framework for Deep [Gaussian Processes](/tags/gaussian-processes/), developed
at Secondmind Labs. It builds on [GPflow](https://www.gpflow.org/) and exposes
Deep GP layers as familiar Keras building blocks, making it easier to compose
deep [probabilistic models](/tags/probabilistic-models/).

Contributed during my doctoral student researcher appointment at Secondmind
Labs, alongside Vincent Dutordoir, ST John, and other members of the lab.
